TITLE: Serum Creatinine - General Guidance
SOURCE STYLE: CDC / ICMR style clinical reference note

Creatinine is a waste product filtered by the kidneys; elevated levels can
indicate reduced kidney function, dehydration, or high muscle turnover.
A single elevated value is often confirmed with a repeat test and an
estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) calculation, since normal
ranges vary with age, sex, and muscle mass. Persistently low creatinine
is less commonly clinically significant and can relate to lower muscle
mass.
