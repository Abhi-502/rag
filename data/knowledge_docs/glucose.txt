TITLE: Fasting Blood Glucose - General Guidance
SOURCE STYLE: WHO / ICMR style clinical reference note

Fasting glucose reflects blood sugar after roughly 8 hours without food.
Values persistently at or above 126 mg/dL on more than one occasion are
generally consistent with diabetes; values between 100-125 mg/dL suggest
prediabetes (impaired fasting glucose) and warrant lifestyle counseling and
follow-up testing such as HbA1c. Low fasting glucose (hypoglycemia) can
cause shakiness, sweating, and confusion, and may relate to medications,
prolonged fasting, or other endocrine conditions.
