TITLE: Red Blood Cell (RBC) Count and Hematocrit - General Guidance
SOURCE STYLE: WHO style clinical reference note

RBC count and hematocrit (the proportion of blood volume made up of red
cells) generally move together with hemoglobin and are used to help
classify anemia (e.g. by red cell size, "MCV") or, when elevated, to
assess for polycythemia. As with hemoglobin, causes range from
dehydration and altitude to underlying bone marrow or lung/heart
conditions, and interpretation should consider the full blood count
together rather than any single value in isolation.
