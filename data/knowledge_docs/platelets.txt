TITLE: Platelet Count - General Guidance
SOURCE STYLE: WHO style clinical reference note

Platelets help blood clot. Low platelet count (thrombocytopenia) can
increase bruising and bleeding risk and may be caused by viral infections,
certain medications, autoimmune conditions, or reduced bone marrow
production. High platelet count (thrombocytosis) can be reactive (after
infection, inflammation, or blood loss) or, less commonly, related to a
bone marrow disorder. Clinical context and trend over time matter more
than a single reading.
