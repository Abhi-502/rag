TITLE: Hemoglobin and Anemia - General Guidance
SOURCE STYLE: WHO / CDC style clinical reference note

Hemoglobin carries oxygen in red blood cells. Low hemoglobin (anemia) can
cause fatigue, pale skin, shortness of breath, and dizziness. Common causes
include iron deficiency, vitamin B12 or folate deficiency, chronic disease,
or blood loss. Mild anemia is often managed with dietary iron and follow-up
testing; more significant or persistent anemia warrants investigation into
the underlying cause, including possible gastrointestinal blood loss in
adults. High hemoglobin (polycythemia) is less common and can relate to
dehydration, smoking, chronic lung disease, or living at high altitude, and
in rarer cases bone marrow disorders.
