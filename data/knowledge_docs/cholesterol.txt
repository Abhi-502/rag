TITLE: Total Cholesterol - General Guidance
SOURCE STYLE: WHO / CDC style clinical reference note

Total cholesterol above 200 mg/dL is generally considered borderline-high,
and above 240 mg/dL high, though clinical risk is better assessed using
the full lipid panel (LDL, HDL, triglycerides) alongside other
cardiovascular risk factors such as blood pressure, smoking status, and
family history. Lifestyle measures (diet, exercise, weight management) are
typically first-line for borderline results.
