TITLE: White Blood Cell (WBC) Count - General Guidance
SOURCE STYLE: CDC / ICMR style clinical reference note

White blood cells are part of the immune system. An elevated WBC count
(leukocytosis) is most often a sign of infection or inflammation, but can
also occur with stress, certain medications (like steroids), smoking, or
less commonly blood cancers. A low WBC count (leukopenia) can result from
viral infections, certain medications, autoimmune conditions, or bone
marrow problems, and may increase infection risk. A single abnormal WBC
value is frequently followed up with a repeat test and a differential
count (breakdown by cell type) before drawing conclusions.
